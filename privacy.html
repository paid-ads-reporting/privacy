Privacy Policy: Paid Ads Reporting

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Paid Ads Reporting is a private internal tool used only by the people who run it. This page explains what it does with the information it can access when you sign in with Google.

What it accesses

  • Google Ads: campaign, ad and search-term reports for ad accounts the signed-in user already has access to. The tool only reads these reports. It does not create, change, pause or delete anything in an ad account, and does not spend money.
  • Basic Google profile: name and email address, used only to sign in to Microsoft Advertising with a Google account.

How it uses that information

  • Reports are copied into a private Google Sheet that only authorized people can open.
  • Search terms and ad text may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to suggest which search terms are irrelevant and to draft ad copy. People review every suggestion before anything is used.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored privately on the operator's computer or in private, encrypted settings. They are never published.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share your information for advertising.
  • It does not collect personal health information.
  • It does not use Google user data to build or train general AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.

Removing access

You can remove this tool's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Reports already copied to the spreadsheet can be deleted on request.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
